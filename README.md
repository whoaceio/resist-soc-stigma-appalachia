# Resisting Social Stigma in Appalachia

What do you think of when you think of Appalachia?\
Who do you think of when you think of Appalachia?

Indigenous, Black, and queer people are often omitted from news and popular narratives of Appalachia.\
When they are mentioned, it's often negatively - in terms of disease and death rates, of exclusion, of a lack of resources - or in the past tense.\
They're framed as people who used to live in the area, but were forced out or eliminated.

This is not true. Indigenous, Black, and queer people are still here.\
Survival, thriving, and celebration are possible for Indigenous, Black, and queer people in Appalachia.\
This map depicts places where presence and celebration occur.
